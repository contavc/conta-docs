Understanding Conta starts with a simple question: **what does it mean to
have control over your money?** To answer it, we'll follow the money: where
you hold it, what gives your balance its value, and what happens when you
save or move it.

You don't need to know about crypto to follow along. Each chapter builds
on the one before it.

## 1. Start with your wallet

Your wallet is the starting point. Learn what non-custodial means and how
your authorisation relates to the money you hold.

[Start with wallets and self-custody →](/en/docs/seguranca)

## 2. Understand your balance

Next, look at the money in that wallet. We introduce balances and
stablecoins, explain why Conta uses cBRL, then meet BRLA and BRS and the
reserves behind them.

[Explore your balance →](/en/docs/dinheiro)

## 3. Set money aside

Once you understand cBRL, a vault is easier to picture: a separate place
for the cBRL you want to save. This is your Reserve in Conta.

[Meet your Reserve →](/en/docs/vaults)

## 4. Move money

Money can arrive in a different asset. Magic Swap connects eligible crypto
to your balance in reais. From there, we look at sending money to others.

[Understand Magic Swap →](/en/docs/pagamentos/magic-swap)
