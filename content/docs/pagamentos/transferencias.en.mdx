---
title: Transfers and money links
description: Ways to send money to other people with Conta.
---

Once money is in your available balance, you can choose where to send it.
Which methods are available depends on account status, limits and the
transaction.
A transfer's destination can be a PIX key, another person on Conta, someone
you share a money link with, or an external wallet. Each option identifies
the recipient in a different way.

## Between people on Conta

A username helps you find the recipient without copying a wallet address.
The transfer connects the sender's assets to the recipient's, with the
movement recorded in the history.

The name makes identification easier. Checking the profile and amount remains
part of authorising the transfer.

## Sending money with a link

A money link represents an amount set aside for someone to claim. The sender
shares the link; the recipient opens it and chooses from the options shown.

The link grants access to the claim, so share it only with the intended
recipient. It is a way of sending value through a conversation or message
without agreeing on every transfer detail in advance.

## Sending crypto to another wallet

You can also send a supported asset, such as digital dollars, to an external address on the network shown in the app. Check the **asset, network, address, amount and quote** before authorising. Your cBRL balance may need to be converted to the asset being sent; the confirmation shows the final value and fees. A wrong address or network may make recovery impossible.

To receive crypto, use the address and network Conta shows for your chosen asset. A supported incoming asset may go through Magic Swap before it appears as a real-denominated balance; not every received token has an available conversion.

## Choosing a method

| Method | How the recipient is identified |
| --- | --- |
| PIX | Through a key or QR code details. |
| Transfer within Conta | Through a profile or username. |
| Money link | Through access to the shared claim link. |
| Crypto to an external wallet | Through the destination wallet's address, asset and network. |

Sending and receiving PIX do not have exactly the same conditions: the app
allows PIX sends without the identity approval used for some other flows,
while an unverified Brazilian account can receive up to **R$500 per deposit**
and **R$1,000 over a rolling 24 hours**. The 24-hour limit also counts pending
PIX deposit requests. An account classified as foreign requires completed
verification to receive PIX. Check the app for your transaction's limits.

The history helps you follow the result. An operation that has started may
still await confirmation before it is complete.

## Following activity with notifications

**App notifications** can alert you to incoming money, conversions, refunds,
payment failures, verification results and claimed money links. You choose
to enable them for each device under **Account → Settings**, where you can
also turn them off. Your browser must allow notifications; on iPhone and iPad,
follow the app's instructions to add it to the Home Screen before enabling them.

A payment notification may show the **amount and sender's name** on the device's
screen. Keep that in mind on a shared device. Notifications help you follow
changes; check the current transaction status in your history before treating
an operation as complete.
