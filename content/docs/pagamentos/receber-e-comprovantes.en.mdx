title: Payment links and receipts
description: Request a payment with your link or a charge, and share a transaction receipt.

You have seen how to send by PIX, to another person on Conta, or with a money link. You can also **request money** with a payment link. The links work in different directions:

| Link | Who creates it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money link | Someone who has set aside money to send. | The recipient opens the link to claim it. |
| Payment link | Someone who wants to receive money. | The payer opens the page to make a payment. |

## Your link and charges

A person with approved identity verification can publish a link tied to their **username**, such as `app.conta.vc/pay/username`. The recipient can share it to request payments; changing the username changes the address too. A payer can open the page and generate a PIX payment without creating a Conta account or signing in. Once confirmed, the recipient receives the value in their real-denominated balance.

A **charge** has its own link with a fixed amount, purpose and expiry. It can be open, paid, expired or cancelled. Unlike the permanent link, the payer does not select the amount of a charge. The owner can follow payment activity and cancel an open charge, according to its state in the app.

Publishing or resuming a link and creating charges requires **approved identity verification**. That rule applies to the public payment page; it does not, on its own, change direct PIX receiving within the applicable limits for an unverified Brazilian account. Available payment methods on the page may vary; check the options actually shown before paying.

## Sharing a receipt

After a transaction, its owner can open the details and share a **receipt**. The `app.conta.vc/receipt/...` link opens a public page without requiring the viewer to sign in. Treat it as shared information: anyone with the address can see the details displayed on the receipt. Check the transaction state in the app, especially while it is processing or being refunded.

The receipt lets people consult and share a record of the transaction. Your account history remains the place to follow updates. Practical app guidance is in the [help centre](https://www.conta.vc/ajuda).
