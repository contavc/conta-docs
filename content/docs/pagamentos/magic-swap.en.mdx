Your Conta balance uses cBRL, but money doesn't always arrive as cBRL.
Someone may send you a dollar stablecoin or another supported crypto
asset instead.

**Magic Swap** is Conta's conversion experience for turning eligible crypto
into a balance in reais. It brings the steps together so you don't have
to arrange each exchange separately.

## From the asset received to cBRL

Think of someone sending you USDC, a dollar stablecoin. That asset starts
with a dollar reference. To become part of your balance in reais, it needs
to be converted at the available exchange rate.

The path may involve moving between networks and exchanging assets.
At the end of a completed conversion, the resulting real-denominated
value is represented by cBRL.

Magic Swap handles the conversion journey. cBRL gives the resulting
balance its consistent form. They play different roles in the same story.

## A quote is an estimate

The app identifies eligible assets and shows an estimated amount in reais
for conversion. What you receive depends on the asset's market value,
the exchange rate, costs and available liquidity.

Liquidity means there are enough assets available to complete an exchange.
Prices can move during execution, so a quote is not a promise of a fixed
final amount, especially for assets with changing market prices.

## Why some assets cannot be converted

An asset needs a supported network and an available conversion path.
Some assets have no usable market; others may be too small in value
to cover conversion costs.

Receiving a token therefore doesn't automatically mean it can become
spendable reais. Use the assets and networks shown as supported in the
app, and follow the conversion status through to completion.

For practical receiving instructions, see
[receiving digital dollars](https://www.conta.vc/receber-dolares).
