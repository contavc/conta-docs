title: Vaults and your Reserve

So far, we've followed cBRL from your wallet to its backing. Now imagine
you want to keep some of that money for later.

A **vault** is a separate place to hold digital assets under defined rules.
In Conta, your personal cBRL vault is presented as your **Reserve**.

## Available to spend, or set aside

Your available balance is the money you use for everyday transactions.
Your Reserve holds the cBRL you've set aside. Moving money between them
changes where it is held, rather than changing it into a different currency.

For example, if you have R$100 available and move R$20 to your Reserve,
you have R$80 available and R$20 saved. You haven't created an extra R$20:
you've separated part of what you already had.

## Connected to your wallet

Each Reserve is associated with its owner's wallet and has its own
address on the network. In its current design, withdrawals return cBRL
to that owner's wallet.

A vault is more than a label on the home screen: it holds a separate
balance that can be checked in public records. It still holds cBRL, so
the backing and dependencies explained in the previous chapter also apply.

## Bringing money back

Withdrawing from your Reserve moves money back to your available Conta
balance. After confirmation, that amount is ready for everyday use again.

There are different ways to build the Reserve, from choosing an amount
yourself to enabling saving rules.
