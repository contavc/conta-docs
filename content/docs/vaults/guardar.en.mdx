Your Reserve holds cBRL separately from your available balance. It is open to eligible accounts; automatic rules begin only after you enable them.

## Choose an amount or enable a rule

You can make a **manual deposit** from your available balance even while Saving Mode is paused. Manual deposits do not earn Match.

With **Saving Mode**, you can turn on PIX payment round-ups and choose a percentage to save on eligible crypto conversions and sends. The rules apply to future completed transactions. On a conversion, the saving comes from the converted amount; on a crypto send, it is added to your debit and shown before confirmation without reducing the amount delivered to the recipient.

A R$12.40 PIX payment, for example, can set aside another R$0.60 from your balance for your Reserve. The recipient still receives R$12.40. Round-ups do not earn Match. Receiving PIX or a transfer between Conta accounts alone does not earn Match either.

## Your savings, Match and interest

| Part | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Your deposits | cBRL you saved manually or through automatic rules. |
| Conta Match | A Conta contribution linked to the percentage saved on eligible crypto activity, subject to program rules and available funding. |
| Interest | A separate Conta-funded credit on the eligible Reserve balance. |

The crypto saving percentage is calculated on the gross conversion amount before the 2.5% fee. Part of that fee funds Match, with no additional reward fee. Early in a cycle, Conta can match an eligible contribution. Once you have saved R$100 of your own money in the cycle, Match+ adds 0.25 percentage points to Conta's contribution rate. Only the first R$500 saved in each 30-day cycle can earn Match; a reward limit and available program funding also apply and are shown in the app. You may keep saving after these limits without new Match.

Each Conta contribution is **pending for 30 days**. That period belongs to each contribution; it does not lock your own savings. Withdrawing early can cancel the pending Match linked to the amount withdrawn, and the app shows the effect before confirmation. Confirmed Match stays yours.

## Referral earnings in your Reserve

If you participate in the referral programme, eligible earnings can be claimed **directly into your Reserve** as cBRL. Earnings generated after the benefit was activated can receive a **25% extra amount on the referral earnings**, funded by Conta, after the supporting principal remains saved for 30 days. Withdrawing that amount early can proportionally cancel the pending extra amount. Earlier earnings do not receive it retroactively.

This extra amount is distinct from Conta Match on conversions and does not consume the cycle's Match limit. The app shows the estimate, pending amounts and applicable conditions before claiming. Referral earnings also depend on programme rules and eligible activity by the person you referred; they are not automatic interest on every Reserve deposit.

## How interest works

The rate currently displayed is **6% annual percentage yield (APY)**, funded by Conta. A vault does not generate interest by itself, and the APY does not automatically come from cBRL's reserves. Manual deposits also count while the program is active; pausing automatic rules does not stop interest on the eligible balance.

Accounting considers the eligible balance and the time it was held during each UTC day. A credit is made at most once a day when the amount owed reaches **R$0.01**; smaller fractions continue accumulating. Earned but uncredited interest is shown separately from the available balance. Check the app for the current rate and terms.

You can withdraw your money to your Conta balance at any time, subject to transaction confirmation. Turning off Saving Mode stops new automatic deposits but leaves what you have already saved in your Reserve.
