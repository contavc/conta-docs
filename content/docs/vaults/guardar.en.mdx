title: Saving and rewards
description: Understand your deposits, optional saving rules and Conta's contributions.
---

Your Reserve gives saved money a place to stay. You decide how to put
money there.

## Choose an amount or enable a rule

You can make a **manual deposit**, moving an amount from your available
Conta balance into your Reserve.

You can also enable **Saving Mode**. Its optional rules can set aside a
percentage of eligible crypto operations or round up PIX payments.
These rules apply after you activate them; having a Reserve does not
activate them for you.

For example, rounding a R$12.40 payment up to the next real sets aside
R$0.60. The saving comes from your money, separate from the payment to
the recipient.

## Your savings and Conta's contributions

It's useful to distinguish three things:

| Part | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Your deposits | Money you moved into the Reserve, manually or through a saving rule. |
| Match | Conta's contribution linked to eligible saving activity, subject to its conditions. |
| Interest | Credits funded by Conta under its savings yield program. |

Putting cBRL in a vault does not, by itself, generate a return. Interest
comes from a separate Conta-funded program. Check the app for its current
rate and conditions; an expected or pending credit is different from
money already credited.

Manual deposits do not earn Match. Eligible automatic savings can earn
it under the program's rules.

## When you need the money

You can request a withdrawal back to your Conta balance. Withdrawing
before related Match is confirmed can cancel that pending contribution.
The app shows the effect when you review the withdrawal.

Turning off future saving rules leaves the money already in your Reserve
there until you choose to withdraw it.
